Privacy Policy for Linden: Menopause Diary & HRT Tracker

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Who we are

Linden: Menopause Diary & HRT Tracker ("Linden") is a Windows app published on the Microsoft Store by batoolash, an individual developer. Contact: batoolashraf.dev@outlook.com (also shown in Linden's About screen, Settings > About, and on Linden's Microsoft Store page).

The short version

Your diary is stored only on your PC. Linden has no account and sends your diary nowhere. The app contacts the Microsoft Store only to check your subscription. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We (the publisher) never receive, see or sell anything you enter.

What Linden stores, and where

Linden asks for your consent on first start before it stores anything. After that it keeps, only in its own app folder on your PC (Windows "LocalState" for the app):

  • your daily entries: symptom ratings, hot flash times and strength, bleeding, notes, triggers you tick and your own symptom names;
  • your medication and HRT list and its change history;
  • your doctor-summary draft (questions, daily-life notes, chosen sections);
  • optional "My situation" details you enter in Settings;
  • app settings (theme, wording, app lock, reminder time, quick log);
  • automatic safety copies of the diary (the last 7 daily copies and copies made before an update or import), also on your PC only.

This is health-related information. It is used only to show your diary, charts and summaries to you. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Network use

Linden does not send your diary over the internet. The only network traffic is the Microsoft Store's own licence and purchase check, made through Windows, to know whether you have a Premium subscription or free trial.

Backups, exports and printing

You can export a backup (.json), a spreadsheet (.csv) or a password-protected backup (.lindenbackup), and print or save a doctor summary as PDF. These files go only where you choose (for example a USB stick, an e-mail you send, or a cloud folder you use); from then on they are under your control and that service's privacy terms. Linden cannot recover the password of an encrypted backup.

Purchases

Subscriptions and free trials are sold and handled by the Microsoft Store. Microsoft processes your payment details under the Microsoft Privacy Statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). Linden only learns whether your subscription or trial is active and when it ends; we never see your name, e-mail or payment details.

Ads, analytics and tracking

None. Linden contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and it never shares data with third parties.

Notifications, app lock and start-up

If you turn on the daily reminder, Windows shows a local notification with neutral text ("Time for your daily check-in"); nothing is sent over the internet. App lock uses Windows Hello on your PC; Linden never receives your face, fingerprint or PIN. Quick log can start Linden in the notification area when you sign in to Windows, only if you turn it on.

Deleting your data

You can change or clear your entries in the app. Uninstalling Linden deletes the diary and its safety copies from your PC; files you exported yourself stay where you saved them.

Security

Your diary is protected by your Windows user account (and BitLocker if your PC uses it). App lock hides your diary from other people using this PC; it does not encrypt the file. Encrypted backups use a password you choose.

Children

Linden is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, and Linden sends no personal information anywhere.

Health disclaimer

Linden is a personal diary for recording how you feel. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and it never suggests or checks medicines or doses. Talk to a doctor or other qualified health professional about your symptoms and treatment. If you feel very unwell, contact your doctor or local emergency services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at the same address with a new "Last updated" date, and Linden's Store listing links to it.
