Privacy Policy for Maker Cost: Handmade Pricing

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who we are

Maker Cost: Handmade Pricing ("Maker Cost") is a Windows app published on the Microsoft Store by batoolash, an individual developer. Contact: batoolashraf.dev@outlook.com (also shown in Maker Cost's About screen, Settings > About, and on its Microsoft Store page).

The short version

Your data stays on your PC. Maker Cost has no account and sends your data nowhere. The app contacts the Microsoft Store only to check your subscription. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We (the publisher) never receive, see or sell anything you enter.

What Maker Cost stores, and where

Maker Cost keeps, only in its own app folder on your PC (Windows "LocalState" for the app):

  • your shop settings and defaults: currency, hourly rate, monthly overhead, items per month, target margin, rounding, units and theme;
  • your materials (name, category, supplier, pack size and price, notes) and their purchase history;
  • your products, recipe lines, prices and fee profiles;
  • stock counts and stock movements, price-rise alerts and yearly cost-of-goods entries (Premium);
  • sales you import from Etsy CSV files (Premium): only the order, transaction and listing numbers, sale dates, item names, SKUs, quantities, prices, order totals, shipping, discounts, card-processing fees and currency. Buyer names, addresses, e-mails, phone numbers and messages in those files are skipped while reading and never stored;
  • automatic safety copies of your data (daily copies for the last 14 days and copies made before an update or restore), also on your PC only;
  • a small file with your subscription state, so Premium works offline.

This information is used only to calculate costs, prices and reports and show them to you. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Network use

Maker Cost does not send your data over the internet. The only network traffic is the Microsoft Store's own licence and purchase check, made through Windows, to know whether you have a Premium subscription or free trial. Links to fee pages (Etsy, Square), to your Microsoft account subscriptions page and to this policy open in your own web browser.

Backups, exports and printing

You can save a backup file (.makercost), export spreadsheets (.csv) and print or save reports and price sheets as PDF. These files go only where you choose (for example a USB stick, an e-mail you send or a cloud folder you use); from then on they are under your control and that service's privacy terms.

Purchases

Subscriptions and free trials are sold and handled by the Microsoft Store. Microsoft processes your payment details under the Microsoft Privacy Statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). Maker Cost only learns whether your subscription or trial is active and when it ends; we never see your name, e-mail or payment details.

Ads, analytics and tracking

None. Maker Cost contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and it never shares data with third parties.

Deleting your data

You can change or delete anything you entered in the app. Uninstalling Maker Cost deletes its data and safety copies from your PC; files you saved yourself stay where you saved them.

Security

Your data is protected by your Windows user account (and BitLocker if your PC uses it). The data file and backups are not encrypted by the app, so keep backup files somewhere safe.

Children

Maker Cost is meant for adults running a small business and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, and Maker Cost sends no personal information anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at the same address with a new "Last updated" date, and Maker Cost's Store listing links to it.
